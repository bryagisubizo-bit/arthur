# Health Information Sources for Arthur

Arthur’s Symptom Support must remain non-diagnostic. It may help a user understand a condition through source-linked public information and a short, plainly labelled summary, but it must not infer a condition from symptoms, assess personal risk, prescribe treatment, or replace emergency or clinical care.

The initial source allow-list is deliberately small and public:

| Source | Intended use | URL |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. National Library of Medicine MedlinePlus | Plain-language condition and medical-encyclopedia pages | https://medlineplus.gov/healthtopics.html |
| U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention | Public disease, condition, and prevention pages | https://www.cdc.gov/health-topics.html |
| United Kingdom National Health Service | Public Health A to Z condition pages | https://www.nhs.uk/health-a-to-z/ |

Arthur must show the selected source name and direct URL before any user-approved browser opening or provider-assisted summary. A condition search with no exact or reviewed source match must say that no reviewed source is available instead of improvising a medical explanation.

## Reviewed source notes

MedlinePlus describes its Health Topics index as covering symptoms, causes, treatment, and prevention for more than 1,000 diseases, illnesses, health conditions, and wellness issues, with regular review and daily link updates. The NHS Health A to Z index offers public pages for conditions, symptoms, medicines, tests, treatments, and guidance about when to get help. Arthur should treat these as information sources only: it must preserve the source link and heading, avoid changing source wording into personal diagnosis, and keep its emergency reminder separate from any condition explanation.

Emergency warning copy remains visible for severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke-like symptoms, severe allergic reaction, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, or immediate safety concerns.
