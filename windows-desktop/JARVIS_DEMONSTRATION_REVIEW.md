## Practical adaptation boundary

Arthur may adopt the following **safe, real-world interaction patterns** from the demonstration: an optional calm arrival greeting; concise spoken status cards; a visual listening/processing/responding indicator; a compact activity timeline; and user-invoked, reviewable workspace cards for permitted local information.

Arthur must not claim cinematic capabilities it does not possess. It will not implement autonomous physical control, security bypass, hidden observation, high-risk automation, fictional hologram claims, or automatic background actions. Any microphone, wake-word, visual result, provider request, or consequential PC action remains user-consented and visibly controlled.
